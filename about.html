About Us

Ready to find your people?

We'd love to welcome you to MOMS Club® of Southeast Aurora, CO.

While many of our activities include children, MOMS Club is ultimately a community for moms. Our goal is to create genuine friendships, offer support through every season of motherhood, and make it easier to feel connected to the people around you.

Our membership area

Where our families live

Our chapter serves families living within these boundaries:

  • N South of Hampden Avenue
  • W East of Parker Road
  • S North of County Line Road
  • E West of the Aurora city limits

Many of our events take place near or around Southlands Mall.

Not sure whether you live within our boundaries? Contact us and we'll be happy to help. If you live outside our area, email support@momsclub.org to find the chapter closest to you.

Membership
