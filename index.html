Find your people

A village close to home

MOMS Club of Southeast Aurora is a local chapter of the International MOMS Club, a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting moms in the communities where they live.

Our chapter welcomes moms within our boundaries who are home during the week, including stay-at-home moms and those who work part-time or run a home-based business. Most activities take place during the day, and children are always welcome unless an event is specifically planned just for moms.

Whether you're new to the area, navigating a new season of motherhood, or simply looking for more connection, there's a place for you at our table.

What we do

Play, connect, and give back

Something for the littles, something just for you, and a whole lot of heart for our community.

Play & Explore

Fun for kids

Playdates, park outings, crafts, coffee club, and other opportunities for kids to play and explore together.

Just for Moms

Connection for moms

Book club, trivia, brunches, moms' nights out, and plenty of casual conversation with women who understand the joy and chaos that is motherhood.

Giving Back

Support for our community

Our chapter participates in service projects benefiting local families and organizations, including the City of Aurora's Welcome Center, school supply drives for local school districts, Parker Task Force during the holidays, the Mother-to-Mother Fund, and many more.

Moments we love

Little adventures, big friendships

Park days, crafts, story time, and the kind of friendships that make the busy seasons feel lighter.
